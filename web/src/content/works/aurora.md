AURORA is an enterprise digital-twin and decision-intelligence platform that models organizations as dependency graphs containing suppliers, factories, products, customers, and technology assets. It provides deterministic what-if disruption simulation, graph analysis, risk scoring, decision approval workflows, and evidence-based AI investigations while keeping calculated business metrics independent from LLM-generated explanations.

The repository explicitly identifies the implemented vertical slice and separates it from future optimization, forecasting, RAG, multi-agent, and broader digital-twin functionality.

## Key Features

- **Digital Twin Modeling**: Graph-based representation of organizational assets
- **Disruption Simulation**: What-if analysis with business impact calculation
- **Risk Scoring**: Weighted risk assessment and recovery strategy evaluation
- **Decision Workflows**: Human-in-the-loop approval processes
- **Graph Intelligence**: BFS/DFS traversal and critical path analysis

## Technologies

ASP.NET Core MVC, C#, Razor Views, PostgreSQL, EF Core, SignalR

BDPriceFinder is a Bangladesh-focused multi-vendor electronics marketplace and price-comparison platform that connects buyers with sellers and vendors. The platform provides product discovery, vendor and product management, product approval workflows, dynamic product variants, inventory and order management, role-based dashboards, authentication, coupons, EMI, warranty information, marketplace search, caching, and vendor-aware product pricing.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Vendor Marketplace**: Support for multiple sellers with vendor-specific dashboards
- **Product Management**: Dynamic variants, inventory tracking, and approval workflows
- **Price Comparison**: Real-time price comparison across vendors
- **Advanced Search**: Elasticsearch-powered product search
- **Business Features**: Coupons, EMI options, warranty management

## Technologies

ASP.NET Core MVC, C#, Razor Views, SQL Server, EF Core, Redis, SignalR, JavaScript

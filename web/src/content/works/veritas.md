VERITAS is a .NET 9 enterprise identity, access-governance, and zero-trust authorization platform designed to determine who can access which resources under specific conditions and provide an auditable explanation for every decision.

It implements multi-tenant authorization, RBAC/ABAC-style policy evaluation, risk scoring, approval workflows, privileged access management, access reviews, auditability, notifications, API-key security, and operator-facing Razor interfaces.

## Key Features

- **Zero-Trust Authorization**: RBAC/ABAC-style policy evaluation with deny-by-default
- **Risk-Based Authentication**: MFA workflows using IP, login failure, and device trust signals
- **Privileged Access Management**: JIT access, access reviews, and approval workflows
- **API Security**: API-key hashing, rotation, and HMAC-SHA256 webhooks
- **Audit Trail**: Comprehensive logging for all access decisions

## Technologies

ASP.NET Core, C#, .NET 9, PostgreSQL, EF Core, Redis, ASP.NET Core Identity, Razor
